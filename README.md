# Superstore-Executive-Dashboard
## Project Objectives
The main objective of this project is to develop an interactive executive-level sales dashboard in Power BI that transforms Superstore sales data into meaningful business insights.

## Dataset used
-<a href="https://github.com/mdwasiquraishi-netizen/Superstore-Executive-Dashboard/blob/main/Superstore%20Dataset.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions
1)-What is the total sales generated by the business?
2)-How many orders have been placed?
3)-How many unique customers are served?
4)-What is the total quantity of products sold?
5)-Which products generate the highest sales?
6)-Which category contributes the most to total sales?
7)-Which region performs best in terms of sales?
8)-Which sub-categories generate the highest and lowest sales?
9)-Which months have the highest sales?
10)-Which months show lower sales performance?
11)-How does sales performance change when filtering by year, region, ship mode, segment, or category?
12)-Which product areas should management focus on to improve overall sales?
